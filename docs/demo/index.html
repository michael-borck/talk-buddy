About the demo: speech recognition runs a small Whisper model right here in your browser (~50 MB, downloaded once and cached — works in every modern browser, audio never uploaded). Being small, it may mishear strong or fast accents — if a transcription misses, just type your answer below. The demo voices are your browser's built-in ones on purpose: the point is practising a conversation, not voice realism. The desktop app adds studio-quality voices, hands-free listening, transcripts, and analysis.
